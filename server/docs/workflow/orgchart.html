Who owns this? Read down a lane. Every piece of work belongs to exactly one department and, within it, to exactly one agent. Work that belongs to nobody does not get done; work that belongs to two gets done twice, and differently.

Where does this stop? Read up. An escalation always goes to the named manager — never sideways, never to “the system”. A specialist that cannot answer hands it to its executive; an executive that cannot hands it to the CEO; the CEO and the Board Chair hand it to you.
