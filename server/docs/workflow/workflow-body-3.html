Stage 3 · Outreach

The first email, and the four that follow it

This is the stage that most obviously pays for the whole system, and the one most able to do damage. Every control in it exists because outbound email done badly costs a sending domain its reputation — which is not recoverable in the same week.

3.1

Enrol in a sequence

Automatic
Starts when
A lead is created, and an active sequence has trigger LEAD_CREATED with a filter the lead matches. Six other triggers exist — see the table below.
Does
Creates an enrolment and schedules step 1 into the next open send slot.
Tools
sequence.enrolsuppression.check
Gate
Level 3. Enrolling somebody is committing to write to them.
Ends when
An enrolment exists at ACTIVE with a nextSendAt.
Stops and asks if
There is no email address, the address is suppressed, or the person is already in this sequence. All three report rather than failing the batch.
3.2

Write the email

Outbound Communications Manager
Starts when
A step comes due and is marked to be written rather than templated.
Does
Drafts from the audit findings and the record — and only from those. The facts it may use are shown to whoever is reading, in the same words the drafter saw, which is what makes the difference between a tailored email and a mail merge visible before it is sent rather than after.
Tools
email.draftlead.readcompany.audit
Gate
Drafting writes nothing outward. It runs at level 1.
Ends when
A subject and a body exist, with the rationale for the angle taken.
Stops and asks if
The facts are thin. The instruction is to write a shorter email, never to fill the space with claims.
3.3

Send it

Clock
Starts when
The minute tick finds a scheduled message whose time has passed, or a due sequence step inside its window.
Does
Re-checks suppression, claims the message as SENDING before the network call, renders on the current letterhead, attaches the logo as an inline part, and sends. A cold approach carries a one-click unsubscribe in its headers; a client being handed their finished website does not.
Tools
email.send
Gate
Level 3. Safe to call twice: a message already SENT returns without doing anything, which is what stops the scheduler and the Send button colliding.
Ends when
Status is SENT with a timestamp, and the send is logged to the lead’s history as a contact.
Stops and asks if
The address was suppressed between drafting and sending — the message is cancelled, not sent and not silently dropped. Suppression is checked at send, never at compose, because somebody can unsubscribe in between.
3.4

Stop the moment they reply

Automatic
Starts when
A reply is logged, an unsubscribe link is clicked, or an address is added to suppression by hand.
Does
Stops every sequence that person is in, not just the one that reached them.
Tools
sequence.stopsuppression.check
Gate
None. Stopping is always allowed, at any level, including 0.
Ends when
Every enrolment for that person is REPLIED or STOPPED.
Stops and asks if
Never. An opt-out is honoured even when the signature does not match — refusing one over a token mismatch is indefensible.

The seven triggers a sequence can start on

TriggerFires onWhat it is for
LEAD_CREATEDA new leadThe cold approach, on a filter — “website missing, score over 60”.
LEAD_STATUS_CHANGEDPipeline movementA lead going QUALIFIED starts a different, warmer series.
PROPOSAL_SENTA proposal going outThe follow-up nobody remembers on day 3 and day 8.
PROJECT_COMPLETEDDelivery finishingThe testimonial ask, and the care-plan conversation.
INVOICE_OVERDUEA due date passingPayment reminders that escalate in tone on a schedule.
CARE_PLAN_REVIEW_DUEA review falling dueBooking the periodic review before the client notices it is late.
MANUALYou enrol themEverything that is a judgement call.

The send window is not a preference

Sequences send inside a window — 08:00–18:00, weekdays only, Africa/Accra by default — computed in the sequence’s own timezone rather than the server’s. A step that comes due at 02:00 on a Sunday waits until Monday morning. Business email arriving at 3am reads as automation, and automation is easier to ignore and easier to report.

Stage 4 · Consultation and proposal

Turning a conversation into a number

The proposal writer is not allowed to invent a price. What it may quote is a fixed catalogue, and everything it says about the reader has to trace to something the audit observed — because a prospect who checks one claim and finds it true believes the rest, and one who finds an invented claim stops reading.

4.1

Book the consultation

Sales Director
Starts when
A lead replies asking for a call, or accepts the meeting request in the sequence.
Does
Reads free time, offers slots, and creates the calendar event with the invitation.
Tools
calendar.readcalendar.write
Gate
Level 3 to actually book. Below it the slots are proposed and the invitation waits.
Ends when
An event exists and the lead has the invitation.
Stops and asks if
The Google connection predates calendar access — reconnecting is the fix, and the refusal says so rather than failing at the moment of booking.
4.2

Write the proposal

Commercial Operations
Starts when
The consultation has happened and the scope is understood.
Does
Writes findings-with-evidence, scope, and an investment table drawn from the published catalogue: website builds from GHS 35,000; care plans at GHS 5,000 / 12,500 / 25,000+ a month. Anything with no published price says the number comes after the call rather than guessing one.
Tools
proposal.draftlead.readcompany.audit
Gate
Spends on Claude, so level 4 to run unattended. Below that it is priced and prepared.
Ends when
A proposal exists at DRAFT with a price, a tier and an expiry.
Stops and asks if
Custom pricing, unclear scope, unusual terms — all three are approval-gated by policy, not by autonomy level. Anything it cannot evidence goes into a list of claims to check rather than into the document.
4.3

Read it and send it

You
Starts when
The draft exists.
Does
You read it. The PDF and the Word cut are rendered on the letterhead; the covering email attaches the proposal, which is rendered at send time so the client receives the document as it stands then, not as it stood when the email was drafted.
Tools
document.renderemail.draftemail.send
Gate
A person, always. A proposal is a commercial commitment under your name.
Ends when
Status is SENT. A PROPOSAL_SENT sequence picks up the follow-up from here.
Stops and asks if
Always. This step is the gate.
4.4

Follow up, then close

Outbound Communications
Starts when
The proposal reaches SENT.
Does
Runs the follow-up series, stopping instantly on any reply. Status moves SENT → VIEWED → ACCEPTED as the client engages.
Tools
sequence.enrolemail.draftsequence.stop
Gate
Level 3 to send.
Ends when
WON — go to Stage 5. Or LOST, with the objection recorded, and the lead becomes eligible for reactivation later.
Stops and asks if
Any negotiation on price. Discounting is never an agent decision.
Stage 5 · Onboarding

From a signature to a plan

The gap between “yes” and “work starting” is where new clients decide whether they made a mistake. Everything here exists to close it inside a day.

5.1

Convert the lead

Commercial Operations
Starts when
A proposal reaches WON.
Does
Creates the client record, links the proposal, and moves the lead to CONVERTED. The lead is not deleted — it is the record of how this client was found.
Tools
lead.updateclient.read
Gate
A write; level 3 to take effect unattended.
Ends when
A client exists with the proposal attached.
Stops and asks if
A client with the same name or domain already exists — merging two client records afterwards is far worse than pausing here.
5.2

Open the project

Delivery Director
Starts when
The client exists.
Does
Creates the project at PLANNING, breaks the accepted scope into milestones and tasks, and assigns each to the specialist whose craft it is — matched against the skills on the roster, not against who is free.
Tools
projects.readtasks.writetime.read
Gate
Writes to tasks; level 3 unattended.
Ends when
Every milestone has tasks, an owner and a date.
Stops and asks if
The scope as written cannot be broken into tasks — that means it was not agreed, it was described.
5.3

Welcome them properly

Client Communications
Starts when
The project reaches PLANNING.
Does
Sends the onboarding email: what happens next, what is needed from them, who they talk to, and when the first thing lands.
Tools
email.draftemail.sendclient.read
Gate
Level 3. Carries no unsubscribe link — a client being onboarded is not being marketed to.
Ends when
The email is SENT and logged against the client.
Stops and asks if
It would name a date the project data does not support. Never promise a date the plan does not contain.
