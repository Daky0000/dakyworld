/ Before you ask

The honest answers.

We already have someone handling IT. Does that rule us out? +

No — it is the most common situation we walk into. Usually that person is capable but stretched across a job that was never meant to include IT. We either take the whole function off them, or take the parts that keep them up at night and leave the rest. Either way they stop being the single point of failure.

Do we have to change the tools we already use? +

Almost never. We work with what you have and connect it properly. A migration only gets recommended when the current setup is actively costing you money or exposing you to risk — and if we recommend one, you get the reasoning in writing before anything moves.

Are you on site, or is this remote? +

Fully remote, across Ghana and West Africa. We handle the systems, not the sockets — no office cabling, printers or hardware repair. Everything we cover is administered digitally, which is also why we can respond inside four hours regardless of where your office is.

How quickly can you start, and how disruptive is it? +

We begin with discovery and a written diagnosis, then take over on staging — never directly on your live systems. You get a short status note every Friday: completed work, next week's plan, blockers. Most clients notice the handover only as things quietly starting to work.

What if we want to stop? +

Retainers run month to month and end on 30 days' written notice. Your data is returned or deleted within 14 days. There is no exit fee and nothing is held hostage — the accounts, domains and systems are yours throughout.
