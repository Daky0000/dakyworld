description: Load the architecture notes for the area you are about to change
---
The architecture notes live in `docs/claude/` and are not loaded automatically.

Look at the index table in CLAUDE.md, pick the file(s) covering $ARGUMENTS, read
them in full, and then summarise only what bears on the change at hand —
including any rule in them that the change would break.

Read more than one file when the change crosses areas. Do not answer from the
index alone; the index is a pointer, not the knowledge.
