The remit

Seven technical functions we manage end to end.

Each one is normally bought from a different supplier. Here, they work together as one function with one accountable technical partner.

01

Websites & web platforms

Your website should generate confidence, enquiries and sales — not create work. We design, build, host and maintain fast, secure websites and web platforms that work properly on every device. From first launch to ongoing improvements, we own the technical work behind your digital front door.

Best for: Company websites, landing pages, e-commerce improvements, ongoing maintenance and performance optimisation.

02

Security & backups

Protect the systems and data your business depends on. We strengthen your digital security, monitor for risks, help respond to incidents and maintain secure off-site backups. If something goes wrong, you have a technical partner who already understands your environment and can act quickly.

Includes: Security reviews, account protection, website hardening, backup management and incident-response support.

03

Email, workspace & cloud

Give every team member the right tools from day one. We set up and manage Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 and the cloud tools that keep your team communicating and working productively. New accounts, secure email, shared files, permissions and day-to-day administration are handled for you.

Includes: User setup, email configuration, shared drives, access controls, migrations and ongoing support.

04

Automation & AI

Remove repetitive work and make your team faster. We identify tasks that consume unnecessary time, then build practical automations and AI-assisted workflows around the way your team already works. The goal is not to add more tools; it is to reduce manual effort and keep operations moving.

Examples: Lead capture and routing, content workflows, follow-ups, reporting, customer updates and internal task handoffs.

05

Integrations & business systems

Make your tools work together. Disconnected software creates duplicate work, missed leads and incomplete information. We connect the systems you rely on — such as your CRM, forms, inbox, calendar, website and WhatsApp — so information moves to the right place automatically.

Includes: CRM setup, lead-routing workflows, reporting flows, app integrations and process mapping.

06

Branding & design

Look as credible as the business you are building. We create clear, practical brand identities that help your business look consistent everywhere customers meet it. Whether you need a logo refresh or a complete identity system, we build assets your team can use confidently.

Includes: Logo design, visual identity, colour and typography systems, templates and brand guidelines.

07

Training & consulting

New technology only matters when your team can use it. We provide hands-on training for the tools, automations and AI workflows we implement. Leadership can also work with us to prioritise technology decisions, improve operations and plan what to build next.

Includes: Team workshops, one-to-one advisory sessions, tool onboarding and workflow reviews.

One partner

One accountable team.

Most businesses buy these services separately: a web developer, a designer, an IT contractor, a security specialist and an automation freelancer. That means more invoices, more handoffs and more chances for important details to be missed.

Dakyworld brings the work under one relationship. You get a team that understands how your website, systems, people and processes connect — plus one clear point of contact and a predictable way to plan your technical spend.

The boundary

Said plainly, so nobody is surprised later.

What we handle — and what we do not.

Dakyworld is a remote technical partner serving businesses across Ghana and West Africa. We deliver our work through chat, calls and screen sharing, allowing us to support clients without requiring an on-site visit.

We focus on digital systems: websites, cloud tools, security, automation, integrations, branding and training. We do not repair physical devices, install office networks, service printers or provide on-site hardware support. If your need sits outside our remit, we will tell you early and clearly.
