How the build works

A clear process, with no guesswork.

We make the work practical, transparent and easy to understand before and during delivery.

01

02

03

What to have ready

You do not need to have everything perfectly organised.

But these items help us scope the work faster and avoid unnecessary questions.

  • 01Your website address and business contact details.
  • 02A list of the tools you use for email, files, accounting, customer management, payments and communication.
  • 03Domain, hosting and website-provider details, if available.
  • 04Information about known issues, such as lost access, slow websites, missing backups or manual processes.
  • 05A list of the staff who need system access.
  • 06Existing brand assets, website content, product information or photographs, where relevant.
  • 07A person in your business who can approve decisions and provide access.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers before you start.

How long does a Foundation Build take?+

Most projects are completed over a defined timeline agreed in the proposal. Timing depends on the project scope, access to existing systems and how quickly approvals or content are provided.

Do we need to sign up for monthly support afterwards?+

No. A Foundation Build is a standalone project. Ongoing support is available if you want Dakyworld to continue managing and improving the systems after handover.

Will we own our website, accounts and data?+

Yes. Your business should retain ownership of its domains, accounts, core files and data. Dakyworld works through controlled access and documents what has been set up.

Can you work with our existing website or tools?+

Usually, yes. The consultation determines whether your current systems can be improved, need restructuring or should be replaced.

What is not included?+

The proposal will state exactly what is included and excluded. Third-party software, subscriptions, hosting, domains, paid plugins and platform fees are normally billed separately unless stated otherwise.
