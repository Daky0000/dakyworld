Before you reach out

Questions?
Good.

A few things people usually want to know before starting a conversation with Dakyworld.

What kind of businesses do you work with?+

We work with established and growing businesses that need dependable technology without the overhead of building a full internal IT team.

Do I need to know exactly what I need?+

No. You can bring us a problem, an idea or a goal. Part of our job is helping you work out what technology solution makes sense.

Can you support an existing website or system?+

Yes. We can work with existing websites, business systems, integrations and workflows, then improve or maintain them where needed.

Do you offer ongoing support?+

Yes. Dakyworld can operate as an outsourced technology department, covering recurring technical needs under an ongoing engagement.

Where are you based?+

Dakyworld is based in Kumasi, Ghana, and works with businesses beyond Ghana through remote delivery.
