Before you choose

The honest answers.

Pricing should be easy to understand. Here are the questions most likely to come up before starting an engagement.

Are the retainers really month to month?+

Yes. Retainers run month to month and end on 30 days' written notice. There is no lock-in or exit fee.

What happens to our data if we leave?+

The published terms state that your data is returned or deleted within 14 days after the engagement ends.

Which plan is right for us?+

Essentials is for teams of 1–15, Growth is for 15–50 staff, and Enterprise Concierge is designed for 50+ staff running platforms that cannot stop.

Can we start with a one-time project?+

Yes. Dakyworld publishes one-time pricing for a Digital Foundation Build, branding, and training and advisory engagements.

Can we discuss a custom arrangement?+

Yes. Enterprise Concierge is already positioned as a custom engagement, and the best way to determine fit is to discuss your systems, team size and operational requirements.
