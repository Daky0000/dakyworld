Common questions

Simple answers before you start.

How do I know whether I need monthly support or a project?+

Choose monthly support if your website, email, cloud systems, security or customer tools need regular attention. Choose a one-time project if you have a defined issue or outcome, such as a website rebuild, email setup, backup setup, brand identity or training session. If you are unsure, book a consultation and we will recommend the right starting point.

Can we start with a project and move to monthly support later?+

Yes. Many businesses begin with a focused project to organise or improve core systems, then move to monthly support when they want ongoing management, protection and improvement.

Do monthly plans require a long contract?+

No. Monthly support runs month to month. You can end your plan with 30 days' written notice.

Will we receive a proposal before work begins?+

Yes. After a consultation, we provide a written proposal that explains the scope, deliverables, responsibilities, price and any relevant third-party costs before work begins.

What happens to our data if we leave?+

Your business keeps ownership of its data and core accounts. At the end of an engagement, we return your data or securely delete it within 14 days, depending on what you choose.
