Common questions

Simple answers before you start.

Are the plans really monthly?+

Yes. Monthly plans run month to month. There is no long-term contract, and you can end your plan with 30 days’ written notice.

What happens to our data if we leave?+

Your business keeps ownership of its data and core accounts. When the engagement ends, we return your data or securely delete it within 14 days, depending on what you choose.

Which plan is right for us?+

Essentials is usually right for small teams that need core systems protected and maintained. Growth is for businesses that also want automation, stronger performance and regular improvement work. Enterprise is for larger or business-critical operations. If you are unsure, book a consultation and we will recommend the right starting point.

Can we start with a one-time project?+

Yes. You can begin with a Digital Foundation Build, branding project, training session or advisory engagement. A monthly retainer is not required before starting a project.

Can we create a custom plan?+

Yes. Enterprise support is tailored to the systems, risks and priorities of your business. For smaller teams, we can also discuss whether a Foundation Build or a defined project is a better first step.
