Takeover

We take over on staging. Never on your live systems.

Handover is the riskiest moment in any technical relationship, so we treat it as one. Access is secured, backups are verified and restored to a staging copy, and the work is proven there before anything touches production.

You get a short status note every Friday throughout — completed work, next week's plan, blockers. Most clients notice the handover only as things quietly starting to work.
