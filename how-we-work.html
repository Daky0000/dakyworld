Discovery Plan Takeover Management
Four stages

From first call to fully managed IT.

Each stage has a clear purpose and a clear result. There are no hidden steps and no confusion about what happens next.

01

Discovery

We learn about your business, your goals and the problems you are facing. We also review your current websites, tools and systems.

We ask questions, check the facts and find out what needs attention. We do not make guesses.

You receive · A clear review of your current setup and a list of our findings.
02

Plan and proposal

We create a plan based on what your business needs. The plan explains what we will manage, what we will improve, how long it will take and what it will cost.

We answer your questions before any work begins.

You receive · A clear proposal and a service agreement.
03

Takeover and setup

We collect the access we need, check your backups and prepare a safe copy of your systems before making major changes.

We test the work before it affects your live website or business systems. You also receive a short update every Friday.

You receive · Your IT systems transferred and ready for us to manage.
04

We manage your IT

Once the setup is complete, we take care of the systems your business depends on.

We monitor your key systems, complete agreed monthly work and meet with you every three months to review progress and plan the next steps.

You receive · Technology managed for you, with less work on your plate.
Safe takeover

We test first. We change later.

Moving from one IT provider to another can be risky. We make the change carefully.

We secure your accounts, check your backups and work on a safe copy of your systems first. We test the changes before they affect your live website or business tools.

Throughout the handover, you receive a short update every Friday. It covers completed work, the plan for the following week and any issues that need your attention.

What is included

Clear service from the start.

Weekly updates

Every Friday, we send a short update covering what we completed, what we plan to do next and any issues we need to discuss.

A signed agreement

Every engagement starts with a signed service agreement. If we will work with private or sensitive information, we can also sign a mutual non-disclosure agreement.

No surprise charges

If you ask for work outside the agreed service, we will explain it, price it and get your approval before we begin.

Response times

You will know when we will respond.

Our response times depend on the type of problem. These times should only be published if they match the service you can reliably provide.

Urgent
Within 4 hours

For a website outage, serious security issue or problem that stops the business from working.

We will contact you, review the issue and begin work to limit the damage.

High priority
Within 6 to 12 hours

For a system that is still working but is slow, damaged or causing problems for your team.

We will either fix the issue or give you a clear plan before the end of the business day.

Normal
Within 24 to 48 hours

For regular requests, small website changes and other tasks that do not stop the business from working.

We will confirm the request and explain when the work will be completed.

Ready when you are

Your technology should work quietly.

You should not have to spend your Monday chasing a developer, checking whether your website is working or worrying about a security issue.

If technology has become another job for you to manage, we can help. We will review your current setup, explain what needs attention and suggest the next step.
