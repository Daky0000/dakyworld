Why we exist

The same room, over and over again.

Most established companies don't have an IT department — and building one makes no sense.

Founder Dan Kwame Ayipah spent five years building websites, fixing security disasters and automating operations for companies across Ghana and West Africa. He kept meeting the same situation: solid, growing businesses where IT was nobody's real job — the office manager, a stretched single hire, or no one at all.

So he built the thing they actually needed: a full IT department they could simply hire.

Today, companies hire Dakyworld to run all their technology — websites, security, email and cloud, automation, systems, branding and training. We've remediated live breaches with zero data loss, cut manual burden by 70%+, and let teams get back to their actual business while we hold up the tech.

The standard

One standard we are judged by: did your business run?

We do not measure ourselves on tickets closed or hours logged. Those describe activity, not outcome, and a business does not care how busy its IT partner was last month.

Zero data-loss incidents across every client we hold, since the day we started. That is the number that matters, and it is the one we are still protecting.
