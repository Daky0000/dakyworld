Services & scope

Dakyworld provides outsourced IT services — web platforms, security, email and cloud administration, automation, AI integration, branding and training — as documented in the signed Service Proposal or Master Service Agreement. Scope is limited to what is documented in writing. Changes require a signed Scope Change Request and may incur additional fees.

02

Fees & payment

Project work: 50% deposit on signing, balance on completion. Retainers: full monthly fee due on the 1st, in advance; non-payment within 15 days may suspend service. Late fees: 1.5% per month on overdue invoices.

03

Currency

International and regional engagements are quoted in US Dollars. GHS contracts are subject to annual review; where the GHS/USD rate moves more than 15% over a 12-month term, either party may request a good-faith adjustment to preserve the agreed value.

Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, Dakyworld is not liable for indirect or consequential damages, lost profits or data, or damages from unauthorised third-party access except where caused by our negligence. In no event shall our total aggregate liability exceed the total fees paid by the Client in the three (3) calendar months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

05

Service levels (retainers)

We provide 24/7 automated monitoring, 365 days a year. For Priority-1 critical incidents (outage or live breach) we guarantee a human response and containment within 4 hours. Priority-2 within 6–12 business hours, routine requests within 24–48 business hours. Security incidents take precedence.

Intellectual property & confidentiality

On full payment, the Client owns all project deliverables. Dakyworld may use anonymised descriptions of the work for case studies unless you opt out in writing, and retains ownership of its pre-existing tools. Both parties keep confidential all sensitive information disclosed during the engagement, except where public or legally required.

07

Force majeure, disputes & termination

Neither party is liable for delays caused by events beyond reasonable control, with notice given within 5 business days. Disputes are first addressed through good-faith negotiation, then mediation or arbitration under the Alternative Dispute Resolution Act, 2010 (Act 798) of Ghana. Retainers may be terminated with 30 days' written notice; on termination we return or delete Client data within 14 days, except as required by law.
