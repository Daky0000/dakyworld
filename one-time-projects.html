Common questions

Simple answers before you start.

Do we need to sign up for monthly support afterwards?+

No. One-time projects are standalone engagements. Ongoing support is available later if you want Dakyworld to continue managing, protecting or improving the systems after handover.

Will we own our website, accounts and data?+

Yes. Your business should retain ownership of its domains, accounts, core files and data. Dakyworld works through controlled access and documents what has been set up.

Can you work with our existing website or tools?+

Usually, yes. The consultation determines whether your current systems can be improved, need restructuring or should be replaced.

What is not included in the displayed price?+

The written proposal states what is included and excluded. Third-party software, subscriptions, hosting, domains, paid plugins, cloud licences and platform fees are normally billed separately unless stated otherwise.

How long does a project take?+

Timing depends on the scope, access to existing systems, the availability of content and how quickly business approvals are provided. Your written proposal will state the expected delivery timeline.
